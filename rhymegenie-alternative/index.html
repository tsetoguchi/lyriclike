A Rhyme Genie alternative that runs in your browser

Rhyme Genie is an app for Mac, iPhone and iPad that finds rhymes. LyricLike is a page you write your lyrics on, in any browser, for free. It checks your syllables and rhyme scheme while you write.

Start in one step

With Rhyme Genie

  1. Use a Mac, iPhone or iPad
  2. Get the app from the App Store
  3. Search for a rhyme
  4. Take it back to your lyrics

With LyricLike

  1. Open the page and write. Any computer or phone, no account needed.

Your verse checks itself

A rhyme finder answers the question you ask it. LyricLike also tells you what you didn't think to ask: a line that runs two syllables long, a rhyme scheme that slipped, or a rhyme hiding inside a line. It's all on the page you're writing.

Built for the writing, not the lookup

Rhyme Genie is a rhyme search first. LyricLike is a writing page first. The rhymes sit next to your lyrics, so you click a word, pick a rhyme, and keep going.

Where Rhyme Genie is deeper

Rhyme Genie sorts rhymes into 30 kinds, finds rhymes that span several syllables, and has a large thesaurus. If you want to dig through every possible rhyme on a Mac, it goes further. For writing the song itself, in any browser, start with LyricLike. It's free to try.
