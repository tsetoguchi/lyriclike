The RhymeBrain alternative built for writing lyrics

RhymeBrain shows you rhymes. LyricLike puts them in your line with one click, counts every syllable and marks your rhyme scheme while you write. Free, no ads, no sign-up.

Click instead of retype

With RhymeBrain

  1. Put your cursor on the word
  2. Find a rhyme in the list
  3. Select the old word
  4. Type the new one

With LyricLike

  1. Click the word. Click a rhyme. It's in your line.

Built for songs, not just words

A rhyme list gives you options. A song needs more: lines that fit the beat, a scheme that holds, and rhymes hiding inside the lines. LyricLike checks all of it on the page you're writing, so you hear problems before you sing them.

Writing in another language?

RhymeBrain covers French, Spanish, German and more. For songs in English, write them in LyricLike.
