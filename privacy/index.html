Privacy

Your lyrics are yours. LyricLike stores them so you can write on any device, and never reads, sells or shares them.

Your lyrics

Without an account, what you write stays in this browser and never leaves the device. With an account, your pages are saved to our database so they open on any device. Nobody looks at them, and they are never used for analytics, advertising or training.

Your account

An account keeps your email address, your name if Google provides one, and your password in hashed form. We can't read the password itself. Signing in sets one cookie that keeps you signed in.

We keep a short log of account events such as sign-ins, sign-ups and password changes. Each entry records the date and a shortened IP address that identifies your network, not you. Entries are deleted after 90 days.

How the app is used

Google Analytics counts visits and which features get used: for example, that a rhyme was tapped or a page was created. When a tapped word has no rhymes, the word itself is sent so the dictionary can be fixed. Nothing you write is ever sent. Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visits apart. Cloudflare Web Analytics also counts visits and page speed, without cookies.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database, and runs Turnstile, which checks that sign-ups come from a person.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" and Google Analytics.
  • Resend sends confirmation and password-reset emails.

Deleting your data

Delete your account from Account → Delete account. That removes your account, every page you saved and your account log right away. Pages kept only in your browser can be cleared by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
