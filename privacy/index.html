Your lyrics

Signed out, your writing stays in this browser and never leaves the device. Signed in, your pages are saved to our database so they open on any device. Nobody looks at them, and they are never used for analytics, advertising or training.

Your account

We store your password only in hashed form, so we can't read it. The account log records events such as sign-ins and password changes. Its IP addresses are cut short, so they point to a network, not a person.

How the app is used

Google Analytics counts visits and which features get used: that a rhyme was tapped, say, or a page was created. When a tapped word has no rhymes, that word is sent so the dictionary can be fixed. Nothing you write is ever sent. Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visits apart. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits and page speed without cookies.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database, and runs Turnstile, which checks that a sign-up comes from a person.
  • Google runs "Continue with Google" and Google Analytics.
  • Resend sends confirmation and password-reset emails.

Deleting your data

Go to Account and choose Delete account. Your account, every saved page and your account log are removed at once. To remove writing kept only in this browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
