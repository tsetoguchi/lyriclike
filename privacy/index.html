Your lyrics

Signed out, your writing stays in this browser and never leaves the device. Signed in, your pages are saved to our database so they open on any device. Nobody looks at them except the people you share a page with, and they are never used for analytics, advertising or training.

Your account

We store your password only in hashed form, so we can't read it. The account log records events such as sign-ins and password changes. Its IP addresses are cut short, so they point to a network, not a person.

Sharing a page

When you share a page, we email the address you type to tell them. The email has your name and the page's title, but none of your words. We keep that address with the page so the person can open it once they log in with it, until the page is deleted or they are taken off it. Everyone on a page can see each other's emails. Every invite has a link to stop these emails; we then keep a scrambled form of the address (a hash, which can't be turned back into it) so we never email it again.

How the app is used

Google Analytics counts visits and which features get used: that a rhyme was tapped, say, or a page was created. When a tapped word has no rhymes, that word is sent so the dictionary can be fixed. Nothing you write is ever sent. Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visits apart. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits and page speed without cookies.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database, and runs Turnstile, which checks that a sign-up comes from a person.
  • Google runs "Continue with Google" and Google Analytics.
  • Resend sends confirmation, password-reset and invite emails.

Seeing and deleting your data

Every page you've saved is in My Notebook, where you can read, edit, rename or delete it. To delete everything, go to Account and choose Delete account. Your account, every saved page, who you shared them with, and your access to pages others shared with you are removed at once, along with your account log. To remove writing kept only in this browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Children

LyricLike is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep accounts for them. If you're under 13, write without an account; your work then stays in your browser.

Where your data is processed

Cloudflare, Google and Resend run servers in the United States and other countries, so your data may be processed outside the country you live in.
