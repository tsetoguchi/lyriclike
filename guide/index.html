How it works

I built LyricLike to count syllables and find rhymes while you write. Almost everything starts with clicking a word.

Find a rhyme

Click or tap any word in your lyrics and its rhymes show up in the panel next to your page. On a phone they're under the Rhymes tab. If you pick a different word, the list changes with it.

Swap one in

Clicking a rhyme puts it in place of the picked word, and it keeps the same capitals. So night becomes light, and NIGHT becomes LIGHT. If no word is picked, clicking a rhyme shows what it means instead.

Look up a meaning

Hover over a rhyme to see what it means. On a phone or tablet, press and hold it.

The six kinds of rhyme

Rhymes are grouped by how close they sound to the word you picked. All the examples below rhyme with light.

  • Perfect Same vowel and the same ending. night
  • Family Same vowel, with an ending that sounds close (t and d, for example). ride
  • Additive Same vowel, with extra sounds at the end. lights
  • Subtractive Same vowel, with fewer sounds at the end. why
  • Assonance Same vowel, but a different ending. time
  • Consonance Different vowel, but the same ending. not

Sort the list

Closest puts the best matches first. Popular puts common words first, A–Z goes in alphabetical order, and Random mixes up the list. If you pick Random again, it changes to Shuffle again and mixes the list one more time.

The sort button is next to the picked word, above the rhymes. It shows which order you're on, and it starts on Closest.

Read the highlights

There are three buttons for the highlights: Syllables, Rhyme Scheme and Internal Rhymes. Syllables and Rhyme Scheme are on when you start, Internal Rhymes is off.

On a computer the buttons are above your page. On a phone, Syllables is in the bottom bar and the other two are under Highlights.

  • Numbers in the margin are the syllable count for each line. This is the Syllables button.
  • Letters at the end of a line show the rhyme scheme. Lines with the same letter rhyme with each other. This is the Rhyme Scheme button.
  • Underlines inside a line mark words that rhyme with another word close by. Words with the same color share a sound. A solid underline means an exact rhyme, and a dashed one means a near rhyme. A word and the same word with something added to the front, like way and away, also get a dashed line, since that's more of a repeat than a rhyme. This is the Internal Rhymes button.

When you pick a word, its group stands out and the rest fade.

Keep your pages

If you're signed out, one page is kept in this browser. If you sign up (it's free), My Notebook keeps all of your pages and they show up on any device. Privacy explains what gets stored.

The notebook button opens My Notebook. It's at the top left on a computer and in the bottom bar on a phone.

Write with others

You can share a page and write it with other people at the same time. Everyone on the page needs a free account.

Press Share and type the email of the person you want to add. They'll get an email with your name and the page title, but none of your lyrics. They can open the page once they log in with that email, or sign up with it if they're new. You can add up to 25 people to one page.

Once someone is on the page, you can also copy the link and send it to them yourself. The link only opens for people you've added.

When other people have the page open, you'll see their cursor and their name as they type, and circles with their initials show who's there. Your changes show up for them right away, and theirs show up for you.

Pages that other people shared with you are under Shared with me in My Notebook. To stop sharing with someone, open Share and remove them. If you were the one added, you can leave the page from the same menu or from My Notebook.

The Share button is right after the page title on a computer, and at the top right above your lyrics on a phone. On a page someone shared with you, it says People and shows who has access.
