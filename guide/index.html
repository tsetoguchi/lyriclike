How it works

LyricLike counts syllables and finds rhymes as you write. It all starts with picking a word.

Find a rhyme

Click or tap any word in your lyrics. Its rhymes appear in the panel beside your page, or under the Rhymes tab on a phone. Pick a different word and the list follows.

Swap one in

Click a rhyme and it replaces the picked word in your lyrics, keeping its capitals: night becomes light, and NIGHT becomes LIGHT. With no word picked, a click shows the rhyme's meaning instead.

Look up a meaning

Rest the pointer on a rhyme to see what it means. On a touch screen, press and hold it.

The six kinds of rhyme

Rhymes are grouped by how closely they sound like the word you picked. The examples all rhyme with light.

  • Perfect Same vowel, same ending. night
  • Family Same vowel, and an ending that sounds close, like t and d. ride
  • Additive Same vowel, with extra sounds on the end. lights
  • Subtractive Same vowel, with fewer sounds on the end. why
  • Assonance Same vowel, a different ending. time
  • Consonance A different vowel, the same ending. not

Sort the list

Closest puts the best matches first. Popular puts common words first, A–Z goes alphabetically, and Random mixes the list. Choose Random again and it says Shuffle again, then reshuffles.

Read the highlights

On a computer, three buttons above your page turn them on and off: Syllables, Rhyme Scheme and Internal Rhymes. On a phone, Syllables is in the bottom bar and the other two are under Highlights. Syllables and Rhyme Scheme start on. Internal Rhymes starts off.

  • Counts in the margin give the syllables in each line.
  • Letters at the end of a line show the rhyme scheme. Lines with the same letter rhyme.
  • Underlines inside a line mark words that rhyme with another word nearby. Words in the same colour share a sound. A solid underline is an exact rhyme, and a dashed one is a near rhyme. A word and its prefixed twin, such as way and away, are dashed too, since they repeat rather than rhyme.
