Delete your account

On the website

You can delete your LyricLike account and everything saved to it at any time. It can't be undone.

  1. Sign in at lyriclike.com.
  2. Open the account menu: the circle with your first letter, top right.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm. If your account has a password, enter it. If it doesn't, type your email address, or type DELETE if you use Sign in with Apple.

In the iOS and Android apps

The steps are the same. Open the app and sign in, tap the circle with your first letter at the top right, then tap Delete account and confirm.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes:

  • Your account: your email address, name and password.
  • Every page you saved, and their earlier versions. Earlier versions are gone within a day.
  • The people you shared your pages with, and your access to pages others shared with you.
  • Your sign-ins on every device, and the ways you sign in, such as Google or Apple.
  • Your account log.
  • Any password reset link or sign-up not yet confirmed.
  • Friend invites you sent or accepted, your invite code, and any page limits set on your account.

Some things stay. Words you wrote on someone else's page stay on that page, without your name. If an address asked us to stop sending invite emails, a scrambled form of it stays, so it is never emailed again. A few small records that can't identify you, kept to stop misuse or to note an error, may also stay for a short time.

Google Analytics never holds your name, email or account, so its visit counts can't be tied to you. They expire after 14 months.

Sign in with Apple

If you used Sign in with Apple, deleting your account also asks Apple to revoke LyricLike's access. LyricLike should then no longer show in your Apple ID's list of apps that use Sign in with Apple.

If you can't sign in

Try resetting your password from the sign-in screen first. If that doesn't work, email support@lyriclike.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. If you used Hide My Email with Apple, say so, and we will help you find the account.

Writing kept only on this device

Writing you did while signed out stays in your browser or the app and was never on our servers. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or remove the app.
