A free MasterWriter alternative for writing lyrics

MasterWriter asks you to buy a license before you write a line. LyricLike opens in your browser for free, with no sign-up, and checks your syllables and rhyme scheme while you write.

Your verse checks itself

A big dictionary helps when you know what you're looking for. LyricLike also tells you what you didn't think to look for: a line that runs two syllables long, a rhyme scheme that slipped, or a rhyme hiding inside a line. It's all on the page you're writing.

Built for the writing, not the lookup

MasterWriter is a reference library first. LyricLike is a writing page first. The rhymes sit next to your lyrics, so you click a word, pick a rhyme, and keep going.

Where MasterWriter is deeper

If you want a thesaurus, a phrase book and a list of figures of speech in one place, MasterWriter has more of them. For writing the song itself, start with LyricLike. It's free to try.
