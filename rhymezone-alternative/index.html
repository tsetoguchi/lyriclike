The RhymeZone alternative built for writing lyrics

RhymeZone makes you leave your song to find a rhyme. LyricLike puts the rhymes right next to your lyrics, counts every syllable and marks your rhyme scheme while you write. Free, no ads, no sign-up.

One click instead of six steps

With RhymeZone

  1. Stop writing
  2. Open a new tab
  3. Type the word
  4. Pick one from the list
  5. Go back to your lyrics
  6. Find your place and type the rhyme

With LyricLike

  1. Click the word. Click a rhyme. It's in your line.

Built for songs, not just words

A rhyming dictionary gives you a list. A song needs more: lines that fit the beat, a scheme that holds, and rhymes hiding inside the lines. LyricLike checks all of it on the page you're writing, so you hear problems before you sing them.
