The best perfect rhymes for “pieces” are ceases, nieces, leases, increases and releases.

“Pieces” /ˈpisəz/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What pieces means

“Pieces” is a form of “piece”.

  • noun a separate part of a whole. an important piece of the evidence
  • verb to join or unite the pieces of.
