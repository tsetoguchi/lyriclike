Words that rhyme with hands

The best perfect rhymes for “hands” are stands, lands, demands, understands and plans.

“Hands” /hændz/ has one syllable.

What hands means

  • noun (with `in') guardianship over; in divorce cases it is the right to house and care for and discipline a child. my fate is in your hands
  • verb place into the hands or custody of. hand me the spoon, please
