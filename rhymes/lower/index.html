The best perfect rhymes for “lower” are slower, mower and blower.

“Lower” /ˈloʊɚ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What lower means

  • adjective less than normal in degree or intensity or amount. low prices
  • verb move something or somebody to a lower position.
