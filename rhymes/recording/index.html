Words that rhyme with recording

The best perfect rhymes for “recording” are according, boarding, regarding, rewarding and hoarding.

“Recording” /rəˈkɔrdɪŋ/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the second.

What recording means

  • verb make a record of; set down in permanent form.
  • noun a signal that encodes something (e.g., picture or sound) that has been recorded.
