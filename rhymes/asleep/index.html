Words that rhyme with asleep

The best perfect rhymes for “asleep” are keep, sleep, deep, bleep and cheap.

“Asleep” /əˈslip/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What asleep means

  • adjective in a state of sleep. were all asleep when the phone rang
  • adverb into a sleeping state. he fell asleep
