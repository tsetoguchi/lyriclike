Words that rhyme with saving

The best perfect rhymes for “saving” are waving, shaving, craving, raving and paving.

“Saving” /ˈseɪvɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What saving means

  • verb save from ruin, destruction, or harm.
  • noun an act of economizing; reduction in cost. there was a saving of 50 cents
