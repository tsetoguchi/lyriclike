Words that rhyme with passes

The best perfect rhymes for “passes” are glasses, classes, masses, gases and grasses.

“Passes” /ˈpæsəz/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What passes means

“Passes” is a form of “pass”.

  • verb go across or through.
  • noun (baseball) an advance to first base by a batter who receives four balls.
