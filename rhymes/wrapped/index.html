Words that rhyme with wrapped

The best perfect rhymes for “wrapped” are trapped, slapped, snapped, tapped and strapped.

“Wrapped” /ræpt/ has one syllable.

What wrapped means

  • verb arrange or fold as a cover or protection. wrap the baby before taking her out
  • adjective covered with or as if with clothes or a wrap or cloak. cloud-wrapped peaks
