Words that rhyme with files

The best perfect rhymes for “files” are miles, smiles, styles, tiles and stiles.

“Files” /faɪlz/ has one syllable.

What files means

“Files” is a form of “file”.

  • verb record in a public office or in a court of law. file for divorce
  • noun a set of related records (either written or electronic) kept together.
