Words that rhyme with jokes

The best perfect rhymes for “jokes” are folks, smokes, stokes, blokes and strokes.

“Jokes” /dʒoʊks/ has one syllable.

What jokes means

“Jokes” is a form of “joke”.

  • noun a humorous anecdote or remark intended to provoke laughter. he told a very funny joke
  • verb tell a joke; speak humorously. He often jokes even when he appears serious
