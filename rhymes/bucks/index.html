The best perfect rhymes for “bucks” are sucks, trucks, ducks, tux and lux.

“Bucks” /bʌks/ has one syllable.

What bucks means

“Bucks” is a form of “buck”.

  • verb to strive with determination.
  • noun a gymnastic horse without pommels and with one end elongated; used lengthwise for vaulting.
