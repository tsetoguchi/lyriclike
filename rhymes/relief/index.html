The best perfect rhymes for “relief” are belief, chief, thief, beef and grief.

“Relief” /rɪˈlif/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What relief means

  • noun the feeling that comes when something burdensome is removed or reduced. as he heard the news he was suddenly flooded with relief
