The best perfect rhymes for “bang” are hang, gang, sang, kang and yang.

“Bang” /bæŋ/ has one syllable.

What bang means

  • verb to produce a sharp often metallic explosive or percussive sound.
  • noun a vigorous blow. he got a bang on the head
