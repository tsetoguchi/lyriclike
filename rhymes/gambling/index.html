The best perfect rhymes for “gambling” are rambling and scrambling.

“Gambling” /ˈgæmbəlɪŋ/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the first.

What gambling means

  • noun the act of playing for stakes in the hope of winning (including the payment of a price for a chance to win a prize). his gambling cost him a fortune
  • verb take a risk in the hope of a favorable outcome. When you buy these stocks you are gambling
