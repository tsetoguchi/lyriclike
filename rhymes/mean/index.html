Words that rhyme with mean

The best perfect rhymes for “mean” are seen, clean, scene, queen and green.

“Mean” /min/ has one syllable.

What mean means

  • verb mean or intend to express or convey. You never understand what I mean!
  • adjective approximating the statistical norm or average or expected value. the mean annual rainfall
