Words that rhyme with solved

The best perfect rhymes for “solved” are involved, evolved, resolved, unsolved and dissolved.

“Solved” /sɑlvd/ has one syllable.

What solved means

  • verb find the solution to (a problem or question) or understand the meaning of. did you solve the problem?
  • adjective explained or answered. mysteries solved and unsolved; problems resolved and unresolved
