“Every” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are treasury, treachery, contrary, canary and remarry.

“Every” /ˈɛvɚi/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the first.

What every means

  • adjective (used of count nouns) each and all of the members of a group considered singly and without exception. every person is mortal
