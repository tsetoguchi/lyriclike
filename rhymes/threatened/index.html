Words that rhyme with threatened

“Threatened” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are second, reckoned, mentioned, legend and questioned.

“Threatened” /ˈθrɛtənd/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What threatened means

  • verb pose a threat to; present a danger to.
  • adjective (of flora or fauna) likely in the near future to become endangered. the spotted owl is a threatened species, not yet an endangered one
