Words that rhyme with held

The best perfect rhymes for “held” are smelled, yelled, spelled, weld and swelled.

“Held” /hɛld/ has one syllable.

What held means

  • verb cause to continue in a certain state, position, or activity; e.g., `keep clean'. hold in place
  • adjective occupied or in the control of; often used in combination. enemy-held territory
