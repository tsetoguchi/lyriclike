“Ostrich” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are lost, crossed, across, cross and cos.

“Ostrich” /ˈɔstrɪtʃ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What ostrich means

  • noun a person who refuses to face reality or recognize the truth (a reference to the popular notion that the ostrich hides from danger by burying its head in the sand).
