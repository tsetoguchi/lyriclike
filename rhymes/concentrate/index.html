Words that rhyme with concentrate

“Concentrate” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are operate, tolerate, compensate, dominate and complicate.

“Concentrate” /ˈkɑnsənˌtreɪt/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the first.

What concentrate means

  • verb make denser, stronger, or purer. concentrate juice
  • noun the desired mineral that is left after impurities have been removed from mined ore.
