Words that rhyme with rooms

The best perfect rhymes for “rooms” are fumes, tombs, blooms, booms and grooms.

“Rooms” /rumz/ has one syllable.

What rooms means

  • noun apartment consisting of a series of connected rooms used as a living unit (as in a hotel).
  • verb live and take one's meals at or in. she rooms in an old boarding house
