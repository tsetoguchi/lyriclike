Words that rhyme with changed

The best perfect rhymes for “changed” are arranged, exchanged, deranged, unchanged and estranged.

“Changed” /tʃeɪndʒd/ has one syllable.

What changed means

  • verb cause to change; make different; cause a transformation. The discussion has changed my thinking about the issue
  • adjective made or become different in nature or form. changed attitudes
