Words that rhyme with blood

The best perfect rhymes for “blood” are bud, mud, flood, thud and stud.

“Blood” /blʌd/ has one syllable.

What blood means

  • noun the fluid (red in vertebrates) that is pumped through the body by the heart and contains plasma, blood cells, and platelets. blood carries oxygen and nutrients to the tissues and carries away waste products
