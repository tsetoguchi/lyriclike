Words that rhyme with needs

The best perfect rhymes for “needs” are leads, reads, seeds, deeds and feeds.

“Needs” /nidz/ has one syllable.

What needs means

“Needs” is a form of “need”.

  • noun a condition requiring relief. she satisfied his need for affection
  • verb require as useful, just, or proper.
