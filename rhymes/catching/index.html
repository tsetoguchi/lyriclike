Words that rhyme with catching

The best perfect rhymes for “catching” are matching, scratching, snatching, patching and hatching.

“Catching” /ˈkætʃɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What catching means

  • verb discover or come upon accidentally, suddenly, or unexpectedly; catch somebody doing something or in a certain state.
  • noun (baseball) playing the position of catcher on a baseball team.
