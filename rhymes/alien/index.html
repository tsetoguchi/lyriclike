“Alien” /ˈeɪliən/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the first.

What alien means

  • adjective not contained in or deriving from the essential nature of something. an economic theory alien to the spirit of capitalism
  • noun a person who comes from a foreign country; someone who does not owe allegiance to your country.
