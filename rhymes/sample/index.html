The best perfect rhymes for “sample” are example, ample and trample.

“Sample” /ˈsæmpəl/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What sample means

  • noun a small part of something intended as representative of the whole.
  • verb take a sample of. Sample the regional dishes
