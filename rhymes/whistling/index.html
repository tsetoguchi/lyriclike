Words that rhyme with whistling

“Whistling” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are sniffling, killing, willing, filling and drilling.

“Whistling” /ˈwɪslɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What whistling means

  • verb make whistling sounds. He lay there, snoring and whistling
  • noun the sound made by something moving rapidly or by steam coming out of a small aperture.
