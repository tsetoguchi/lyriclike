The best perfect rhymes for “above” are of, love, shove, thereof and glove.

“Above” /əˈbʌv/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What above means

  • adverb (in writing) at an earlier place. see above
  • adjective appearing earlier in the same text. flaws in the above interpretation
