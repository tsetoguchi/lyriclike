Words that rhyme with hammer

The best perfect rhymes for “hammer” are glamour, grammar, amor, slammer and jammer.

“Hammer” /ˈhæmɚ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What hammer means

  • noun the part of a gunlock that strikes the percussion cap when the trigger is pulled.
  • verb beat with or as if with a hammer. hammer the metal flat
