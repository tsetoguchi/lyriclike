Words that rhyme with process

“Process” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are progress, compress, across, boss and cross.

“Process” /ˈprɑˌsɛs/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What process means

  • verb subject to a process or treatment, with the aim of readying for some purpose, improving, or remedying a condition. process cheese
  • noun a particular course of action intended to achieve a result. it was a process of trial and error
