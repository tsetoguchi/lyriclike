Words that rhyme with pulling

“Pulling” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are looking, during, cooking, pushing and pudding.

“Pulling” /ˈpʊlɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What pulling means

  • verb cause to move by pulling. pull a sled
  • noun the act of pulling; applying force to move something toward or with you. his strenuous pulling strained his back
