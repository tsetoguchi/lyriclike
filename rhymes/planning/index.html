Words that rhyme with planning

The best perfect rhymes for “planning” are manning, scanning, tanning, banning and canning.

“Planning” /ˈplænɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What planning means

  • verb have the will and intention to carry out some action.
  • noun an act of formulating a program for a definite course of action. the planning was more fun than the trip itself
