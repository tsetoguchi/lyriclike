Words that rhyme with leaves

The best perfect rhymes for “leaves” are thieves, believes, sleeves, reeves and grieves.

“Leaves” /livz/ has one syllable.

What leaves means

  • verb go away from a place. At what time does your train leave?
  • noun the main organ of photosynthesis and transpiration in higher plants.
