Words that rhyme with tears

The best perfect rhymes for “tears” are cares, stairs, bears, wears and prayers.

“Tears” /tɛrz/ has one syllable.

What tears means

  • verb separate or cause to separate abruptly. tear the paper
  • noun the process of shedding tears (usually accompanied by sobs or other inarticulate sounds). she was in tears
