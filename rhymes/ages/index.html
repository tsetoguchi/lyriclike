Words that rhyme with ages

The best perfect rhymes for “ages” are pages, wages and stages.

“Ages” /ˈeɪdʒəz/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What ages means

“Ages” is a form of “age”.

  • noun how long something has existed. it was replaced because of its age
  • verb begin to seem older; get older. The death of his wife caused him to age fast
