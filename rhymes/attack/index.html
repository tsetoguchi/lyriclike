The best perfect rhymes for “attack” are back, black, jack, track and pack.

“Attack” /əˈtæk/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What attack means

  • noun (military) an offensive against an enemy (using weapons). the attack began at dawn
  • verb launch an attack or assault on; begin hostilities or start warfare with.
