“Headed” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are credit, edit, discredit, intrepid and setup.

“Headed” /ˈhɛdəd/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What headed means

  • verb to go or travel towards. We were headed for the mountains
  • adjective having a heading or course in a certain direction. westward headed wagons
