Words that rhyme with visit

The only perfect rhyme for “visit” is revisit.

“Visit” /ˈvɪzɪt/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What visit means

  • verb go to see a place, as for entertainment.
  • noun the act of going to see some person or place or thing for a short time. he dropped by for a visit
