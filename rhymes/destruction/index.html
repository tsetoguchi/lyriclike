Words that rhyme with destruction

The best perfect rhymes for “destruction” are production, construction, abduction, instruction and obstruction.

“Destruction” /dɪˈstrʌkʃən/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the second.

What destruction means

  • noun the termination of something by causing so much damage to it that it cannot be repaired or no longer exists.
