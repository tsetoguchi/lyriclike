The best perfect rhymes for “testing” are suggesting, resting, nesting, arresting and requesting.

“Testing” /ˈtɛstɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What testing means

  • noun the act of subjecting to experimental test in order to determine how well something works. they agreed to end the testing of atomic weapons
  • verb put to the test, as for its quality, or give experimental use to. Test this recipe
