Words that rhyme with beginning

The best perfect rhymes for “beginning” are winning, spinning, grinning, inning and pinning.

“Beginning” /bɪˈgɪnɪŋ/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the second.

What beginning means

  • noun the event consisting of the start of something. the beginning of the war
  • verb take the first step or steps in carrying out an action.
