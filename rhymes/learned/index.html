Words that rhyme with learned

The best perfect rhymes for “learned” are turned, burned, earned, concerned and returned.

“Learned” /lɝnd/ has one syllable.

What learned means

  • verb gain knowledge or skills. She learned dancing from her sister
  • adjective having or showing profound knowledge. a learned jurist
