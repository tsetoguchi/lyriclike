Words that rhyme with going

The best perfect rhymes for “going” are knowing, showing, growing, throwing and blowing.

“Going” /ˈgoʊɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What going means

  • verb change location; move, travel, or proceed, also metaphorically. How fast does your new car go?
  • noun the act of departing.
