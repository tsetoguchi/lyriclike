“Joined” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are point, appoint, disappoint, boyfriend and poisoned.

“Joined” /dʒɔɪnd/ has one syllable.

What joined means

  • verb become part of; become a member of a group or organization. He joined the Communist Party as a young man
  • adjective of or relating to two people who are married to each other.
