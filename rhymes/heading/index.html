Words that rhyme with heading

The best perfect rhymes for “heading” are wedding, spreading, shedding, bedding and treading.

“Heading” /ˈhɛdɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What heading means

  • verb to go or travel towards. where is she heading
  • noun a line of text serving to indicate what the passage below it is about. the heading seemed to have little to do with the text
