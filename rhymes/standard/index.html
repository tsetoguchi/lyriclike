“Standard” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are tampered, pampered, anchored, angered and hamburg.

“Standard” /ˈstændɚd/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What standard means

  • adjective conforming to or constituting a standard of measurement or value; or of the usual or regularized or accepted kind. windows of standard width
  • noun a basis for comparison; a reference point against which other things can be evaluated.
