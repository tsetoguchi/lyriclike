Words that rhyme with counting

The best perfect rhymes for “counting” are mounting and accounting.

“Counting” /ˈkaʊntɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What counting means

  • verb determine the number or amount of. Can you count the books on your shelf?
  • noun the act of counting; reciting numbers in ascending order. the counting continued for several hours
