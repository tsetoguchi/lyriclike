“Yesterday” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are anyway, getaway, expressway, already and ready.

“Yesterday” /ˈjɛstɚˌdeɪ/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the first.

What yesterday means

  • noun the day immediately before today.
  • adverb on the day preceding today. yesterday the weather was beautiful
