Words that rhyme with downtown

The best perfect rhymes for “downtown” are around, down, town, brown and crown.

“Downtown” /ˈdaʊnˈtaʊn/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What downtown means

  • noun the central area or commercial center of a town or city. the heart of Birmingham's downtown
  • adjective of or located in the lower part of a town, or in the business center. downtown Manhattan
