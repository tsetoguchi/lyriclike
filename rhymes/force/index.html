Words that rhyme with force

The best perfect rhymes for “force” are course, horse, source, divorce and coarse.

“Force” /fɔrs/ has one syllable.

What force means

  • noun a powerful effect or influence. the force of his eloquence easily persuaded them
  • verb to cause to do through pressure or necessity, by physical, moral or intellectual means.
