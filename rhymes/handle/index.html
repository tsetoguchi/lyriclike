The best perfect rhymes for “handle” are scandal, candle, sandal, crandall and vandal.

“Handle” /ˈhændəl/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What handle means

  • verb be in charge of, act on, or dispose of. This blender can't handle nuts
  • noun the appendage to an object that is designed to be held in order to use or move it. he grabbed the hammer by the handle
