The best perfect rhymes for “duty” are beauty, booty, cutie, fruity and tutti.

“Duty” /ˈduti/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What duty means

  • noun the social force that binds you to the courses of action demanded by that force. we must instill a sense of duty in our children
