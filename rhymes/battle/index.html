The best perfect rhymes for “battle” are cattle and rattle.

“Battle” /ˈbætəl/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What battle means

  • noun a hostile meeting of opposing military forces in the course of a war. Grant won a decisive victory in the battle of Chickamauga
  • verb battle or contend against in or as if in a battle.
