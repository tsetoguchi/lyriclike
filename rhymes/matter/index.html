Words that rhyme with matter

The best perfect rhymes for “matter” are chatter, flatter, batter, clatter and scatter.

“Matter” /ˈmætɚ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What matter means

  • noun a vaguely specified concern.
  • verb have weight; have import, carry weight. It does not matter much
