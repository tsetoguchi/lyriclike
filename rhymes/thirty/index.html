The best perfect rhymes for “thirty” are dirty, birdie, sturdy, nerdy and gertie.

“Thirty” /ˈθɝˌdi/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What thirty means

  • noun the cardinal number that is the product of ten and three.
  • adjective being ten more than twenty.
