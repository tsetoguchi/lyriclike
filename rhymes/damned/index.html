The best perfect rhymes for “damned” are jammed, slammed, crammed and rammed.

“Damned” /dæmd/ has one syllable.

What damned means

  • adjective expletives used informally as intensifiers. I'll be damned (or blessed or darned or goddamned) if I'll do any such thing
  • noun people who are condemned to eternal punishment. he felt he had visited the realm of the damned
