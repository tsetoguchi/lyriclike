Words that rhyme with sets

The best perfect rhymes for “sets” are gets, lets, threats, cigarettes and debts.

“Sets” /sɛts/ has one syllable.

What sets means

“Sets” is a form of “set”.

  • verb put into a certain place or abstract location. Set the tray down
  • noun a group of things of the same kind that belong together and are so used. a set of books
