The best perfect rhymes for “keeps” are beeps, sleeps, creeps, sweeps and leaps.

“Keeps” /kips/ has one syllable.

What keeps means

“Keeps” is a form of “keep”.

  • verb cause to continue in a certain state, position, or activity; e.g., `keep clean'. The students keep me on my toes
