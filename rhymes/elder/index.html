“Elder” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are shelter, helper, elders, elderman and elderly.

“Elder” /ˈɛldɚ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What elder means

  • noun a person who is older than you are.
  • adjective used of the older of two persons of the same name especially used to distinguish a father from his son.
