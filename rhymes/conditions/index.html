Words that rhyme with conditions

The best perfect rhymes for “conditions” are positions, musicians, traditions, suspicions and auditions.

“Conditions” /kənˈdɪʃənz/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the second.

What conditions means

“Conditions” is a form of “condition”.

  • noun a state at a particular time. a condition (or state) of disrepair
  • verb establish a conditioned response.
