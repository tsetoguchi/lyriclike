Words that rhyme with worth

The best perfect rhymes for “worth” are earth, birth, berth and rebirth.

“Worth” /wɝθ/ has one syllable.

What worth means

  • noun an indefinite quantity of something having a specified value. 10 dollars worth of gasoline
  • adjective (often used ironically) worthy of being treated in a particular way. an idea worth considering
