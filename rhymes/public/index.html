“Public” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are publicly, republicans, luck, club and truck.

“Public” /ˈpʌblɪk/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What public means

  • noun people in general considered as a whole. he is a hero in the eyes of the public
  • adjective not private; open to or concerning the people as a whole. the public good
