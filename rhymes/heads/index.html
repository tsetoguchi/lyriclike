Words that rhyme with heads

The best perfect rhymes for “heads” are beds, feds, spreads, reds and threads.

“Heads” /hɛdz/ has one syllable.

What heads means

“Heads” is a form of “head”.

  • noun the upper part of the human body or the front part of the body in animals; contains the face and brains. he stuck his head out the window
  • verb to go or travel towards.
