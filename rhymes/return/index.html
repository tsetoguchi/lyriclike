Words that rhyme with return

The best perfect rhymes for “return” are concern, turn, learn, burn and earn.

“Return” /rɪˈtɝn/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What return means

  • verb go or come back to place, condition, or activity where one has been before. return to your native land
  • noun document giving the tax collector information about the taxpayer's tax liability. his gross income was enough that he had to file a tax return
