Words that rhyme with uses

The best perfect rhymes for “uses” are loses, excuses, bruises, chooses and juices.

“Uses” /ˈjusəz/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What uses means

“Uses” is a form of “use”.

  • noun the act of using. he warned against the use of narcotic drugs
  • verb put into service; make work or employ for a particular purpose or for its inherent or natural purpose. use your head!
