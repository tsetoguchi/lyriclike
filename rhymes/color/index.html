Words that rhyme with color

The best perfect rhymes for “color” are dollar, smaller, taller, muller and waller.

“Color” /ˈkʌlɚ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What color means

  • noun a visual attribute of things that results from the light they emit or transmit or reflect. a white color is made up of many different wavelengths of light
  • verb add color to.
