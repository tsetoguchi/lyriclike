“Thousand” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are thousands, around, found, sound and round.

“Thousand” /ˈθaʊzənd/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What thousand means

  • noun the cardinal number that is the product of 10 and 100.
  • adjective denoting a quantity consisting of 1,000 items or units.
