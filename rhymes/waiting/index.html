Words that rhyme with waiting

The best perfect rhymes for “waiting” are dating, creating, hating, skating and mating.

“Waiting” /ˈweɪtɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What waiting means

  • verb stay in one place and anticipate or expect something. I had to wait on line for an hour to get the tickets
  • noun the act of waiting (remaining inactive in one place while expecting something).
