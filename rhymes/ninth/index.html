Words that rhyme with ninth

“Ninth” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are lines, signs, times, crimes and sometimes.

“Ninth” /naɪnθ/ has one syllable.

What ninth means

  • noun position nine in a countable series of things. going into the ninth they were a run ahead
  • adjective coming next after the eighth and just before the tenth in position.
