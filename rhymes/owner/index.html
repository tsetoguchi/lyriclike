Words that rhyme with owner

The best perfect rhymes for “owner” are donor, loner, kroner and stoner.

“Owner” /ˈoʊnɚ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What owner means

  • noun (law) someone who owns (is legal possessor of) a business. he is the owner of a chain of restaurants
  • adjective belonging to or on behalf of a specified person (especially yourself); preceded by a possessive. for your own use
