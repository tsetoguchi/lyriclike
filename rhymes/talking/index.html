Words that rhyme with talking

The best perfect rhymes for “talking” are walking, knocking, stalking, squawking and hawking.

“Talking” /ˈtɔkɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What talking means

  • verb exchange thoughts; talk with. We often talk business
  • noun an exchange of ideas via conversation.
