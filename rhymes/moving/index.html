The best perfect rhymes for “moving” are proving, removing and improving.

“Moving” /ˈmuvɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What moving means

  • verb change location; move, travel, or proceed, also metaphorically.
  • adjective arousing or capable of arousing deep emotion. she laid her case of destitution before him in a very moving letter
