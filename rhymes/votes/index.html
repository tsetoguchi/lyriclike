“Votes” /voʊts/ has one syllable.

What votes means

“Votes” is a form of “vote”.

  • noun a choice that is made by counting the number of people in favor of each alternative. there were only 17 votes in favor of the motion
  • verb express one's preference for a candidate or for a measure or resolution; cast a vote.
