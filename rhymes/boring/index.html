Words that rhyme with boring

The best perfect rhymes for “boring” are roaring, snoring, pouring, scoring and soaring.

“Boring” /ˈbɔrɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What boring means

  • verb cause to be bored.
  • adjective so lacking in interest as to cause mental weariness. a boring evening with uninteresting people
