Words that rhyme with mighty

The best perfect rhymes for “mighty” are whitey, righty, almighty and alrighty.

“Mighty” /ˈmaɪti/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What mighty means

  • adjective having or showing great strength or force or intensity. struck a mighty blow
  • adverb (Southern regional intensive) very; to a great degree. the baby is mighty cute
