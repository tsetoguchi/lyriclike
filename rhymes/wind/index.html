The best perfect rhymes for “wind” are find, kind, mind, blind and signed.

“Wind” /waɪnd/ has one syllable.

What wind means

  • noun air moving (sometimes with considerable force) from an area of high pressure to an area of low pressure. when there is no wind, row
  • verb to move or cause to move in a sinuous, spiral, or circular course.
