Words that rhyme with extreme

The best perfect rhymes for “extreme” are supreme, team, seem, dream and cream.

“Extreme” /ɛkˈstrim/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What extreme means

  • adjective of the greatest possible degree or extent or intensity. extreme cold
  • noun the furthest or highest degree of something.
