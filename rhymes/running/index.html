Words that rhyme with running

The best perfect rhymes for “running” are stunning, cunning and gunning.

“Running” /ˈrʌnɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What running means

  • verb move fast by using one's feet, with one foot off the ground at any given time. Don't run--you'll be out of breath
  • noun (American football) a play in which a player attempts to carry the ball through or past the opposing team. the coach put great emphasis on running
