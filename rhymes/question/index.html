Words that rhyme with question

The best perfect rhymes for “question” are session, impression, confession, possession and expression.

“Question” /ˈkwɛstʃən/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What question means

  • verb challenge the accuracy, probity, or propriety of. We must question your judgment in this matter
  • noun an instance of questioning. there was a question about my training
