Words that rhyme with bad

The best perfect rhymes for “bad” are had, dad, glad, mad and sad.

“Bad” /bæd/ has one syllable.

What bad means

  • adjective having undesirable or negative qualities. a bad report card
  • adverb with great intensity (`bad' is a nonstandard variant for `badly'). it hurts bad
