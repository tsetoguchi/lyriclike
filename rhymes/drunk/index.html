The best perfect rhymes for “drunk” are trunk, monk, punk, junk and bunk.

“Drunk” /drʌŋk/ has one syllable.

What drunk means

  • verb take in liquids. The patient must drink several liters each day
  • adjective stupefied or excited by a chemical substance (especially alcohol).
