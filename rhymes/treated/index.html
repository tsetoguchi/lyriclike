The best perfect rhymes for “treated” are cheated, completed, defeated, seated and heated.

“Treated” /ˈtritɪd/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What treated means

  • verb interact in a certain way. Treat him with caution, please
  • adjective subjected to a physical (or chemical) treatment or action or agent. the sludge of treated sewage can be used as fertilizer
