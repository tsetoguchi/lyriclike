The best perfect rhymes for “yelling” are telling, selling, smelling, swelling and spelling.

“Yelling” /ˈjɛlɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What yelling means

  • verb utter a sudden loud cry.
  • noun uttering a loud inarticulate cry as of pain or excitement.
