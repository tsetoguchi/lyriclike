The best perfect rhymes for “signs” are lines, mines, shines, whines and pines.

“Signs” /saɪnz/ has one syllable.

What signs means

“Signs” is a form of “sign”.

  • noun a perceptible indication of something not immediately apparent (as a visible clue that something has happened). he showed signs of strain
  • verb mark with one's signature; write one's name (on). Please sign here
