Words that rhyme with help

The best perfect rhymes for “help” are yelp and kelp.

“Help” /hɛlp/ has one syllable.

What help means

  • verb give help or assistance; be of service. Can you help me carry this table?
  • noun the activity of contributing to the fulfillment of a need or furtherance of an effort or purpose. offered his help in unloading
