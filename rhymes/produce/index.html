Words that rhyme with produce

The best perfect rhymes for “produce” are excuse, abuse, use, introduce and loose.

“Produce” /prəˈdus/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What produce means

  • verb bring forth or yield. The tree would not produce fruit
  • noun fresh fruits and vegetable grown for the market.
