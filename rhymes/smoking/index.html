Words that rhyme with smoking

The best perfect rhymes for “smoking” are joking, choking, poking, soaking and croaking.

“Smoking” /ˈsmoʊkɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What smoking means

  • noun the act of smoking tobacco or other substances. smoking stinks
  • verb inhale and exhale smoke from cigarettes, cigars, pipes. Do you smoke?
