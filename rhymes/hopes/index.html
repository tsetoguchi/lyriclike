The best perfect rhymes for “hopes” are ropes, slopes and soaps.

“Hopes” /hoʊps/ has one syllable.

What hopes means

“Hopes” is a form of “hope”.

  • noun a specific instance of feeling hopeful. it revived their hope of winning the pennant
  • verb expect and wish. I hope she understands that she cannot expect a raise
