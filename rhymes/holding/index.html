The best perfect rhymes for “holding” are folding, scolding, molding, withholding and unfolding.

“Holding” /ˈhoʊldɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What holding means

  • verb cause to continue in a certain state, position, or activity; e.g., `keep clean'. hold in place
  • noun the act of retaining something.
