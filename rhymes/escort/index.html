Words that rhyme with escort

The best perfect rhymes for “escort” are report, support, transport, sort and court.

“Escort” /ɛˈskɔrt/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What escort means

  • noun someone who escorts and protects a prominent person.
  • verb accompany as an escort. She asked her older brother to escort her to the ball
