“Breathing” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are leaving, teaching, screeching, freezing and reaching.

“Breathing” /ˈbriðɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What breathing means

  • verb draw air into, and expel out of, the lungs. I can breathe better when the air is clean
  • noun the bodily process of inhalation and exhalation; the process of taking in oxygen from inhaled air and releasing carbon dioxide by exhalation.
