Words that rhyme with completely

The best perfect rhymes for “completely” are discreetly, neatly and sweetly.

“Completely” /kəmˈplitli/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the second.

What completely means

  • adverb to a complete degree or to the full or entire extent (`whole' is often used informally for `wholly'). it was completely different from what we expected
