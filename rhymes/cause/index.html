Words that rhyme with cause

The best perfect rhymes for “cause” are was, laws, because, applause and oz.

“Cause” /kɑz/ has one syllable.

What cause means

  • noun events that provide the generative force that is the origin of something. they are trying to determine the cause of the crash
  • verb give rise to; cause to happen or occur, not always intentionally. cause a commotion
