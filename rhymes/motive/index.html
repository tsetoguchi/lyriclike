The best perfect rhymes for “motive” are locomotive and automotive.

“Motive” /ˈmoʊtɪv/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What motive means

  • noun the psychological feature that arouses an organism to action toward a desired goal; the reason for the action; that which gives purpose and direction to behavior.
  • adjective causing or able to cause motion. a motive force
