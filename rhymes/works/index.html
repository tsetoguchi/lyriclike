The best perfect rhymes for “works” are jerks, turks, perks, clerks and lurks.

“Works” /wɝks/ has one syllable.

What works means

  • verb exert oneself by doing mental or physical work for a purpose or out of necessity. I will work hard to improve my grades
  • noun buildings for carrying on industrial labor.
