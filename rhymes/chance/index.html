Words that rhyme with chance

The best perfect rhymes for “chance” are dance, lance, advance, romance and glance.

“Chance” /tʃæns/ has one syllable.

What chance means

  • noun a possibility due to a favorable combination of circumstances. now is your chance
  • verb be the case by chance.
