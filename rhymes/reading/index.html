Words that rhyme with reading

The best perfect rhymes for “reading” are wedding, heading, bleeding, leading and feeding.

“Reading” /ˈridɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What reading means

  • verb interpret something that is written or printed. read the advertisement
  • noun the cognitive process of understanding a written linguistic message. his main reading was detective stories
