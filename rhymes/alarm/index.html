Words that rhyme with alarm

The best perfect rhymes for “alarm” are arm, harm, farm, charm and perform.

“Alarm” /əˈlɑrm/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What alarm means

  • noun fear resulting from the awareness of danger.
  • verb fill with apprehension or alarm; cause to be unpleasantly surprised.
