“Opposite” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are composite, consulate, moderate, nominate and obstinate.

“Opposite” /ˈɑpəzət/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the first.

What opposite means

  • adjective being directly across from each other; facing. And I on the opposite shore will be, ready to ride and spread the alarm
  • noun a word that expresses a meaning opposed to the meaning of another word, in which case the two words are antonyms of each other.
