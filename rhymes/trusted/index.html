The best perfect rhymes for “trusted” are busted, dusted, rusted, entrusted and adjusted.

“Trusted” /ˈtrʌstɪd/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What trusted means

  • verb have confidence or faith in. We can trust in our government
  • adjective (of persons) worthy of trust or confidence. a sure (or trusted) friend
