Words that rhyme with least

The best perfect rhymes for “least” are east, priest, beast, feast and released.

“Least” /list/ has one syllable.

What least means

  • adjective the superlative of `little' that can be used with mass nouns and is usually preceded by `the'; a quantifier meaning smallest in amount or extent or degree. didn't care the least bit
  • adverb used to form the superlative. The garter snake is the least dangerous snake
