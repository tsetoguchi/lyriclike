Words that rhyme with yawn

The best perfect rhymes for “yawn” are on, gone, dawn, von and drawn.

“Yawn” /jɔn/ has one syllable.

What yawn means

  • noun an involuntary intake of breath through a wide open mouth; usually triggered by fatigue or boredom. he could not suppress a yawn
  • verb utter a yawn, as from lack of oxygen or when one is tired.
