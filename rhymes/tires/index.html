The best perfect rhymes for “tires” are fires, requires, wires, liars and buyers.

What tires means

“Tires” is a form of “tire”.

  • verb lose interest or become bored with something or somebody.
  • noun hoop that covers a wheel. automobile tires are usually made of rubber and filled with compressed air
