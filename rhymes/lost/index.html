Words that rhyme with lost

The best perfect rhymes for “lost” are crossed, cost, frost, tossed and exhaust.

“Lost” /lɔst/ has one syllable.

What lost means

  • verb fail to keep or to maintain; cease to have, either physically or in an abstract sense. She lost her purse when she left it unattended on her seat
  • adjective no longer in your possession or control; unable to be found or recovered. a lost child
