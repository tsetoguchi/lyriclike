The best perfect rhymes for “sticks” are six, fix, clicks, tricks and mix.

What sticks means

“Sticks” is a form of “stick”.

  • noun an implement consisting of a length of wood. he collected dry sticks for a campfire
  • verb put, fix, force, or implant. stick your thumb in the crack
