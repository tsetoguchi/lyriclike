The best perfect rhymes for “drops” are cops, stops, pops, shops and tops.

What drops means

“Drops” is a form of “drop”.

  • verb let fall to the ground. Don't drop the dishes
  • noun a shape that is spherical and small. he studied the shapes of low-viscosity drops
