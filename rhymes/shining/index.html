Words that rhyme with shining

The best perfect rhymes for “shining” are dining, signing, whining, mining and lining.

“Shining” /ˈʃaɪnɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What shining means

  • verb be bright by reflecting or casting light.
  • noun the work of making something smooth and shiny by rubbing or waxing it. the shining of shoes provided a meager living
