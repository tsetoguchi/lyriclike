Words that rhyme with dying

The best perfect rhymes for “dying” are trying, lying, crying, flying and buying.

“Dying” /ˈdaɪɪŋ/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What dying means

  • verb pass from physical life and lose all bodily attributes and functions necessary to sustain life.
  • noun the time when something ends. a dying of old hopes
