Words that rhyme with engaged

The best perfect rhymes for “engaged” are enraged, aged, staged, paged and caged.

“Engaged” /ɛnˈgeɪdʒd/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What engaged means

  • verb carry out or participate in an activity; be involved in. They engaged in a discussion
  • adjective having ones attention or mind or energy engaged. deeply engaged in conversation
