Words that rhyme with sometimes

The best perfect rhymes for “sometimes” are times, crimes, chimes, rhymes and grimes.

“Sometimes” /səmˈtaɪmz/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the second.

What sometimes means

  • adverb on certain occasions or in certain cases but not always. sometimes she wished she were back in England
