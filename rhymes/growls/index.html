The best perfect rhymes for “growls” are howls and owls.

“Growls” /graʊlz/ has one syllable.

What growls means

“Growls” is a form of “growl”.

  • noun the sound of growling (as made by animals).
  • verb to utter or emit low dull rumbling sounds.
