The best perfect rhymes for “common” are uncommon and lawman.

“Common” /ˈkɑmən/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What common means

  • adjective belonging to or participated in by a community as a whole; public. for the common good
  • noun a piece of open land for recreational use in an urban area.
