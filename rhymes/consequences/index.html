“Consequences” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are audiences, conferences, accomplices, response and upon.

What consequences means

“Consequences” is a form of “consequence”.

  • noun a phenomenon that follows and is caused by some previous phenomenon. his decision had depressing consequences for business
