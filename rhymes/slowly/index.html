Words that rhyme with slowly

The best perfect rhymes for “slowly” are holy, lowly, wholly, goalie and moly.

“Slowly” /ˈsloʊli/ has 2 syllables, stressed on the first.

What slowly means

  • adverb without speed (`slow' is sometimes used informally for `slowly'). he spoke slowly
