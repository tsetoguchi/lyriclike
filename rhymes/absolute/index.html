“Absolute” has no perfect rhymes in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. The closest near rhymes are attribute, that, at, cat and grab.

“Absolute” /ˈæbsəˌlut/ has 3 syllables, stressed on the first.

What absolute means

  • adjective perfect or complete or pure. absolute loyalty
  • noun something that is conceived or that exists independently and not in relation to other things; something that does not depend on anything else and is beyond human control; something that is not relative. no mortal being can influence the absolute
